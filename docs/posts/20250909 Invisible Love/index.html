I love you so —

as you love me,

in the evening and in the morning.

There are so many reasons I cannot love you openly,

and that only deepens the sorrow inside me.

The cruelest of them all is this:

you do not love me in return.

I never asked you to stay by my side,

and yet you need not leave —

I will love you quietly, unseen.

I am so sad; my tears show it.

Yesterday felt like love, today feels like farewell.

I was crying with a smiling face,

and the reason I weep so sadly now

is the longing I know will come tomorrow.

(Repeated)

I never asked you to stay,

but there’s no need for you to go.

I’ll love you without being seen.

Yesterday was love; today is parting —

I cried with a smiling face,

because what I feel now will turn into longing by tomorrow.

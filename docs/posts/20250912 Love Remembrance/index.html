사랑하는 사람을 보내야만 했던 모든사람에게 바칩니다

돌아보면 너무나 아름다웠어 내 인생에 다시 못올 순간들이었어

Thus sang a popular group from the early part of this century.

looking back, it was all so magical moments that will not return for all those that had to say goodbye to loved ones.

The song crescendos to these words,

사랑해 그리고 기억해 I recall and love you 떠난게 후회될때 언제라도 whenever you regret leaving, 나의 품에 돌아와도 되 you can return

Though this and many other popular songs are about 연인 (愛人) or lovers, the meaning or message can be carried to familial love as well.

Koreans use degrees of kinship 촌수 (寸數)1 when referring to relatives.

  • Parent to child (1 촌寸)
  • Siblings (2 촌寸)
  • Grandparents (2 촌寸)
  • Aunts and Uncles (3 촌寸)
  • Great-Grandparents (3 촌寸)
  • Cousins (4 촌寸)

Grateful that I had felt love from the immediate family and beyond. And the continuation of that relationship and cultivation of that love

However, I have also seen strifes among siblings, especially after a marriage.

Hoping that a change will happen and love and trust can be restored.

Our Heavenly Father, is a loving being Wishes for us to return and fully participate.

We can return when we feel that we have departed far from the path.

This is for all who’ve had to say goodbye

to the one they loved.

Looking back, it was all so beautiful,

moments in my life that will never come again.

When my eyes met yours, when I watched you,

tears would fall for no reason at all.

and I knew you were not my own.

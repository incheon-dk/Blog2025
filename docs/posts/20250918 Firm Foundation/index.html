A hymn, first published in 1787, in a book titled A Selection of Hymns from the Best Authors and attributed to John Keene is sung often.

Especially in large gatherings as a congregational song.1

One time, a group of young people sang the hymn in 4 languages.

Starting with Mandarin, followed by Japanese, then Korean.

Closing with everyone singing the hymn in English.

須聽主今説道，你不要懼怕，

我永遠護庇你在翅膀之下。

總賜你有力量能站立得住，

我全能的右手，常將你保護，

我全能的右手，常將你保護。

恐るな，われは汝なが神　常に汝なんじと共にあり

助け与え，強くして

너희 주 하나님 나 함께 하리라

힘 주고 도우어 높이 세우리라

전능한 손으로 전능한 손으로

내 손이 높이 널 받들겠노라

Fear not, I am with thee; oh, be not dismayed,

For I am thy God and will still give thee aid.

I’ll strengthen thee, help thee, and cause thee to stand,

Upheld by my righteous, upheld by my righteous,

Upheld by my righteous, omnipotent hand.

Drove to where the Aspen trees outnumber the Pine trees.

Looking for the spread of fall colors at 8000 feet level (2400 Meters)

Stayed long enough to get requisite shots with a DSLR and a phone.

Eyes were saturated by hues and shades.

Near the turn for the trails that lead to Mount Timpanogos, saw the light on a tree.

Nearby was tree that had only its roots.

Hidden from the road and had an unsightly view, above ground

Nearby, there were other tree remnants with a network of roots, supporting the first tree that was visible and lit by the light.

There are days and times that seem especially long and difficult.

Yet, looking back, there has been outcomes that are hard to explain in earthly terms.

Financial burdens are lifted by unexpected means of reduction or sources of income.

Job opening and subsequent offer is shared from a surprising source.

Serendipitous meeting with a person, that continues into courtship and marriage.

Though it is none seen, we are aided in this life by forces.

Those forces or agents are those that have completed their earthly mission.

Together with our Father, they are active in and present to,

strengthen thee, help thee, and cause thee to stand,

Thank you Mom and Dad, and Grand Parents.

I feel your strength.

1.

How firm a foundation, ye Saints of the Lord,

Is laid for your faith in his excellent word!

What more can he say than to you he hath said,

Who unto the Savior, who unto the Savior,

Who unto the Savior for refuge have fled?

2.

In ev’ry condition—in sickness, in health,

In poverty’s vale or abounding in wealth,

At home or abroad, on the land or the sea—

As thy days may demand, as thy days may demand,

As thy days may demand, so thy succor shall be.

3.

Fear not, I am with thee; oh, be not dismayed,

For I am thy God and will still give thee aid.

I’ll strengthen thee, help thee, and cause thee to stand,

Upheld by my righteous, upheld by my righteous,

Upheld by my righteous, omnipotent hand.

4.

When through the deep waters I call thee to go,

The rivers of sorrow shall not thee o’erflow,

For I will be with thee, thy troubles to bless,

And sanctify to thee, and sanctify to thee,

And sanctify to thee thy deepest distress.

5.

When through fiery trials thy pathway shall lie,

My grace, all sufficient, shall be thy supply.

The flame shall not hurt thee; I only design

Thy dross to consume, thy dross to consume,

Thy dross to consume and thy gold to refine.

6.

E’en down to old age, all my people shall prove

My sov’reign, eternal, unchangeable love;

And then, when gray hair shall their temples adorn,

Like lambs shall they still, like lambs shall they still,

Like lambs shall they still in my bosom be borne.

7.

The soul that on Jesus hath leaned for repose

I will not, I cannot, desert to his foes;

That soul, though all hell should endeavor to shake,

I’ll never, no never, I’ll never, no never,

I’ll never, no never, no never forsake!

One of the roles of a chosen generation is not only to develop a new technology, the ones that will lead to less burdensome life for future generations, but to demonstrate how to properly use that new technology.

How did my parents navigate their world? It was simpler, yet what tool, knowledge guided How did they know if their decision was good or off the mark.

They learned from experiences Lessons learned wasn’t something taught at school. It was their textbook for life. They were able to discern based on varied and deep association with fellow human beings.

Their teachings were based on their experience and designed to lessen our trials. Not repeating mistakes they made.

Pitfalls of high interest rates Dangers of smoking and drinking Gambling woes.

Those were more visible, tangible ones.

What were some of the less visible woes.

Then one, born in 1949, took on the challenge of explaining Deep Learning in the Age of AI

Though I was pulling for him. Wondered if a person in his mid 70s could properly understand and address this topic.

How to benefit from technology not swallowed by it.

I was sorely off the mark.

He not only understood the challenges we face but offered an elegant, time tested solution to our dilemma.

He began by graciously acknowledging and praising the host.

Then, referring to the audience, who were mostly in their 20s, he said.

It’s a great season in the Lord’s work, and I am grateful to be with you today…Many years ago, I had a strong visual impression about you and about this day… and I knew that the Lord would bless [you] with opportunities for deep learning, spiritually and temporally, and raise [you] up as a mighty army to build His kingdom and prepare the earth for His return. You are those children…This is your day. It is a great day in the kingdom of God. The Lord is preparing His kingdom and His people for His return, and you are playing a central role in that great work. The Lord Jesus Christ is hastening His work, and this is a day of miracles. An increasing flow of new technologies are making it possible for learning and education to flourish in both secular and spiritual knowledge. The Lord is moving with power in His church, all across the earth, to help you obtain the education He wants you to have. That education requires deep learning, learning of the whole soul, heart, mind, body, and your eternal spirit. The Lord is preparing wonderful opportunities for you to experience deep learning, but they come with important challenges.

He then presented this approach,

The Elements of Deep Learning is,

  • Know
  • Feel
  • Do
  • Become

Application to the Principle of Repentance

  • To know in your mind the principles of repentance
  • To understand and feel repentance in your heart
  • To take effective, righteous action by applying the principles of repentance in the Lord’s way
  • To become more like Jesus Christ and our Heavenly Father
